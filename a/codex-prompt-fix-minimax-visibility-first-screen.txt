## Context — the actual problem to solve

There are currently two "sample" entry points on the Welcome screen:

1. **"Try a sample itinerary"** — a structured-data shortcut. It loads a pre-built itinerary
   directly and INTENTIONALLY SKIPS extraction. Because of this, MiniMax M3 always shows
   "offline" on this path. This is correct/honest behavior given how the shortcut works, but
   a judge clicking the FIRST or most obvious sample button and seeing "MiniMax M3: offline"
   will very plausibly read that as a broken integration, not as intentional design — the app
   gives them no context to tell the difference.
2. **"Try sample itinerary screenshot"** — a second, less prominent option that actually runs
   the real upload → extraction (MiniMax) → review → confirm pipeline, and should show MiniMax
   as genuinely live.

The core risk: if judges only click the first, more prominent button (which is the natural
thing to do), they will never see MiniMax do anything, and will see it reported as "offline"
— directly undermining a hackathon requirement to show the AI model's role clearly and make it
"visibly essential" (this framing appears in prior project judging-criteria notes). This is
a serious risk, not a cosmetic one: it could read as a non-functional or fake integration.

2. Confirm there is no way today for a judge who only clicks the obvious/first button to see

### Step 2 — Decide and specify the fix: which path should be primary

Evaluate and recommend ONE of these approaches (do not implement — just recommend with
reasoning, as this is a product decision Cursor will build exactly as specified):

**Option A — Promote the screenshot/extraction demo to be the primary, most prominent sample
button**, since it is the one that actually demonstrates MiniMax. Demote (but keep) the
structured-data shortcut as a secondary "or, skip straight to results" fast-path option for
users who don't care about seeing extraction.

**Option B — Merge the two into one flow**: the "Try a sample itinerary" button always runs
through real extraction (using the sample screenshot under the hood) rather than loading
structured data directly, so there is only ONE sample entry point and it always demonstrates
MiniMax. Evaluate whether this adds unacceptable latency/friction to the fast-demo path
compared to the current instant structured-data shortcut, and weigh that against the risk of
a judge never seeing MiniMax run at all.

**Option C — Keep both paths, but make the choice and its consequence explicit and impossible
to miss**: relabel the buttons so a judge cannot mistake which one shows AI extraction (e.g.
"Try a sample itinerary — instant, skips AI extraction" vs. "Try a sample itinerary — see
MiniMax M3 extract it live"), and ensure the provider-status bar/copy explicitly explains WHY
MiniMax shows offline on the fast path rather than leaving it unexplained.

State your recommendation with clear reasoning about hackathon-judging risk versus demo speed,
referencing the actual judging-criteria concern (AI model must be "visibly essential," judges
should see it early, per prior project notes) as the deciding factor. Given the stated goal —
judges must see MiniMax actually extract data, and must never be able to mistake correct
behavior for a bug — weight your recommendation toward whichever option makes that
unambiguous, even if it costs a small amount of demo speed.
