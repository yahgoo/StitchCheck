## What StitchCheck is (context)

A hackathon travel app: upload a flight screenshot (or use a sample), extract the itinerary
via MiniMax M3, evaluate self-transfer connection risk, show real flight alternatives from
Atlas Sandbox (read-only, no booking/payment ever). Two Welcome-screen demo paths exist:
"Use the ready-made sample — skips extraction" and "Try the screenshot sample — extract with
MiniMax M3." Both should reach Options with live Atlas alternatives and complete to Done.

## Known fixed issues this session (do not rediscover, but DO re-verify live now that Atlas works)

1. Atlas CLI secure-store contention (`SECURE_STORE_UNAVAILABLE`) — pre-existing macOS
   keychain flakiness, not app code.
2. Concurrent Atlas calls on Options entry (`SERVICE_TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE`) — real
   regression, fixed by serializing the primary search and the unbooked-previews effect in
   `app/src/App.tsx`.
3. Airport-mismatch bug — Atlas sometimes substituted nearby airports (e.g. SZB/DMK) instead
   of the exact requested origin/destination. Fixed with `filterOffersForRequestedRoute()` in
   `app/server/atlas-proxy.mjs` (~line 197), applied in `handleSearch` before responses reach
   the browser. Independently re-verified as present and effective on all Atlas-calling
   browser code paths (main alternatives list AND per-leg unbooked previews).
