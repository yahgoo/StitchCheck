# Research Brief

## Audience
Kimi, Z.ai, and ChatGPT. Each model independently completes this brief.

## Objective
Identify evidence-backed pain points faced by budget travellers and generate
three distinct hackathon product ideas per model (nine ideas total across the
three models).

## Mandatory Idea Requirements
Every proposed idea must:
- Fit exactly one official Build with Gemini hackathon theme.
- Give Gemini an essential and visible role.
- Give Atlas Flight Booking Sandbox an essential and visible travel-data or
  travel-action role.
- Give Nosana an essential and visible workload/deployment role whose output
  the app consumes.
- Be feasible as a working vertical slice before Saturday, 22 August 2026.
- Avoid real payments, production bookings, and real personal data.
- Include a safe, repeatable demo path and failure fallback.

## Required Fields for Every Idea
Each of the three ideas must provide all twelve items:
1. Name and one-sentence pitch.
2. Target budget-traveller segment.
3. Specific pain point and trigger moment.
4. Evidence and source URLs.
5. Existing workaround and why it fails.
6. Official hackathon theme selected and justification.
7. Gemini role and visible demo evidence.
8. Atlas Sandbox role and visible demo evidence.
9. Nosana role and visible demo evidence.
10. Primary user flow: trigger -> decision -> outcome.
11. Smallest P0 vertical slice.
12. Key risks, assumptions, and fallback path.

## Final Instruction
Do not recommend a winner. NotebookLM will compare all nine ideas after
importing these outputs and their cited sources.
