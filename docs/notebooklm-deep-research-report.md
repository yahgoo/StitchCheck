5. Winning Recommendation
We are thrilled to recommend StitchCheck (Origin: research-kimi.md) as the unanimous
winning idea 13. It passes every hard gate flawlessly, stands on an unshakeable, verified
evidence base, and leverages the entire tech stack with unparalleled elegance.
1. Idea Name & Source Model: StitchCheck by Kimi 13.
2. Selected Hackathon Theme: Most Creative Gemini Hack (Official Track) 9, 14.
3. One-Sentence Pitch: Screenshot your cheap separate tickets before paying, and
StitchCheck instantly builds a narrated "failure-cascade timeline" to expose your real
missed-connection risks and book a safer alternative in the sandbox 13.
4. Why the User Pain is Real (Independent Evidence):
5. Extreme vulnerability of self-connections: A minor flight delay of just 30 minutes
causes 40% of self-connecting passengers to miss their onward segments 27, 29.
6. No legal fallback: Point-to-point contracts on separate tickets are completely exempt
from through-ticket rebooking and care protections under both EU261 and US DOT rules
26, 52, 53.
7. Punitive "No-Show" cascades: Missing the first leg frequently triggers "no-show"
clauses, allowing low-cost carriers to cancel the traveler’s entire return itinerary without
refunds 27, 30.
8. High financial harm: Stranded travelers face average financial losses of €770 per
disrupted booking when airlines deny care and rerouting 27, 31.
9. Gemini Role & Visible Proof:
10. Role: Gemini (Flash-class) performs multimodal extraction on screenshots of separate
checkouts/tickets to build a structured itinerary JSON, then maps these flights against
the destination airport’s minimum connection layout to generate a spoken
"failure-cascade narrative" 14, 48.
11. Visible proof in demo: The UI displays the original ticket screenshots side-by-side with a
rendered gate-to-gate transit schedule and the Gemini-narrated timeline of what
happens minute-by-minute if Leg 1 is delayed 48.
12. Atlas Sandbox Role & Visible Proof:
13. Role: Atlas searches real flight shopping tables to retrieve safer, alliance-linked, or
protected connecting alternatives, and handles the mock-booking checkout 48.
14. Visible proof in demo: A priced table of Atlas alternative itineraries is displayed (e.g., the
risky separate-ticket connection vs. a safer protected connection), and a sandbox
booking confirmation screen appears when the traveler decides to secure the flight 48.
15. Nosana Role & Visible Proof:
16. Role: Executes high-throughput batch Monte Carlo reliability simulations on route-pair
delay data to serve a risk-score API. This is a perfect workload for decentralized GPUs
as it involves heavy numerical calculations with zero privacy risks (no PII involved) 54.
17. Visible proof in demo: A live status panel displays the Nosana Job ID, GPU node details,
and the finalized connection risk score rendered in the UI 54.
18. P0 Vertical Slice (Single User Journey):
19. One User: Synthetic traveler profile ("Demo Traveler / demo@example.com") 55.
20. One Trigger: Traveler uploads screenshots of two unbooked, tight separate-ticket
checkouts 54.
21. One Decision: StitchCheck reveals the 75-minute connection has a 40% risk score 29,
54, and presents a safer protected route for only €14 more 16. The user must decide to
risk it ("Keep") or choose safety ("Switch") 54.
22. One Outcome: The user clicks "Switch" to buy the safer option, triggering a
human-confirmed sandbox flight booking receipt 48, 54.
23. Explicit Non-Goals:
24. No integration with real payment cards or production checkout gateways.
25. No live, dynamic real-time prediction of weather/delays (uses a static historical dataset)
49.
26. No actual production bookings or passenger seat reservations on live flights.
27. No storage or processing of real personal identifying information (PII).
28. Three Highest-Risk Assumptions to Smoke-Test First:
29. Screenshot OCR Robustness: Gemini's ability to accurately extract exact
departure/arrival times, airport codes, and carrier names across highly fragmented layout
variations of various airline checkout pages 49.
30. Historical Data Coverage: That the historical delay dataset used by the Nosana batch job
has sufficient coverage for secondary LCC airports to produce meaningful risk heuristics
49.
31. Atlas Sandbox API Latency: That the Atlas Flight Booking Sandbox API responds
reliably and quickly under hackathon network constraints to return alternative priced
offers 49.
