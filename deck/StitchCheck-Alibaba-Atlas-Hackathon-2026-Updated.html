The Traveler Problem

A tight connection can turn a cheap trip into a missed-flight cost

  • Two separate tickets, one fragile chain. If the first flight runs late, the second ticket is the traveler's problem — no airline owes a rebooking.
  • The price you saw isn't the price you get. Fares drift between searching and booking, sometimes within minutes.
  • The risk is invisible at checkout. Booking sites show the cheapest option, never "will this connection fail you?"
  • The way out is one search away — but never shown. Single-ticket alternatives that remove the risk entirely rarely appear at the moment of decision.

Reliability & Safety

Forgiving on flakiness, strict on anything that writes

  • One-click Verify, resilient underneath. Search and Verify retry transient provider availability issues (e.g. SERVICE_TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE) automatically while the UI is loading — no silent dead ends.
  • Fail-closed safety gates protect order, payment, and ticketing steps. A 7-layer gate chain — all seven must pass before any sandbox write executes; every flag defaults to off:
Ticketing availabilityAuthorizationRoute requirementsSandbox modeSandbox base-URL exact matchPayment confirmationTicketing confirmationVerification
  • Production and sandbox writes are mutually exclusive. The production write kill switch stays off — enforced and covered by the offline gate test suite.
  • No real payment or ticket issuance. Everything past Verify happens in the Atlas Sandbox test environment.
